Singback

Sing against a target note and watch a live pitch trace, or read a script aloud and see which words landed. Every number is measured in your browser from your own microphone.

Your microphone is used only in this tab. Audio is not uploaded; only a short clip goes to the transcription model when you ask for a reading.

    Singback is front-end only. Pitch is measured locally with the Web Audio API and the YIN algorithm; the transcript and the coaching tip come from Pollinations models paid for from your own Pollen balance.

    How this app calls Pollinations

    You sign in with Pollinations (OAuth code flow with PKCE, so nothing is pasted and the token stays in this tab's sessionStorage). A take produces one POST https://gen.pollinations.ai/v1/audio/transcriptions with the recorded clip, then one POST https://gen.pollinations.ai/v1/chat/completions to openai/gpt-5.4-nano for the single coaching line. Both models are served with paid_only: false, so a take costs a fraction of a Pollen from your own balance. The app requests the audio and text model scopes on sign-in.
